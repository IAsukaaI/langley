ABOUT LANG!LEY
Lang!ley is a lightweight, 100% Open Source Windows Application that brings better looking, real-time, Cross-Platform Friendlists, and Media Controls directly to your Discord Rich Presence.

OPEN BETA & DEVELOPMENT STATUS
Lang!ley is currently in Open Beta and in active Development. Because we are constantly expanding Platform Support and adding new Features, some Bugs or small Issues can still happen while using the App.

FEATURES & CAPABILITIES
• Fortnite Real-Time Outfit, Match State & Level Sync: Instantly detects your equipped Fortnite Skin (with exact Skin Icon), Lobby/In-Match State, active Playlist, Season Level, Party Size, and elapsed Session Time.
• Automatic Lang!ley Squad Detection: Detects Teammates in your Fortnite Party who also run Lang!ley (★ Lang!ley Squad) via Epic Games Party Metadata without any external Servers.
• Major Minecraft Clients Support: Automatically detects Lunar Client, Badlion Client, Feather Client, LabyMod 4, Prism Launcher, Vanilla Java & Bedrock including your active Minecraft Version and World/Multiplayer Server State.
• Discord #1 Priority Guard: Prevents other Apps from hijacking or overwriting your Discord Rich Presence.
• Unified Game Hub & Live CS2 / Dota 2 GSI Stats: Browse your installed Steam, Xbox Game Pass, and Launcher Libraries in one unified Tab, Launch or Close Steam Games with 1 Click, and show live Match Stats (Map, Score, K/D/A) in Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2.
• Cross-Platform Friends Tab: Check Friends & Statuses across Epic, Steam, Roblox, Xbox Live, Discord, Riot Games, EA, Ubisoft, GOG, and Battle.net.
• Media & Spotify Controller: Built-in playback Controls supporting Spotify, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Apple Music, and Browser Players with optional live Song Display inside your Discord Status across all Games.
• Ultra-Low Resource Usage (<70MB RAM): Powered by a shared, TTL-cached OS Process & Window Scanner so all Detectors share a single lightweight scan per cycle with zero FPS Drops.

SECURITY & PRIVACY INFORMATION
• 100% Open Source: Lang!ley is completely Open Source on GitHub. Everyone can inspect the Code by themselves to verify that nothing shady happens in the Background.
• Optional Account Linking: Linking Accounts is completely Optional. Detection for Games works right out of the Box using local Logs, Manifests, and Registry Keys.
• 100% Local Storage on Your PC: All your Settings and Data stay locally on your PC (...\AppData\Roaming\Lang!ley) and never get shared with anyone (even the Lang!ley Team cant see anything of you).
• Zero External Backend Servers: Lang!ley has no external Backend Server and never collects, tracks, or sells Personal Data. All requests go directly between your PC and Official Game / Discord APIs.
• Official Browser Login & Safe Linking: If you decide to connect your Accounts, you sign in directly on the Official Websites of the Launchers in your Browser. Lang!ley never sees your Passwords or 2FA Codes.
• Instant Unlink & Per-Platform Toggles: You can unlink any Account or toggle individual Games on or off at any time with a single Click.

WHY DID WE BUILD LANG!LEY?
Normally, default Discord Rich Presence for Games, looks either super basic or is completely missing. That's why we built Lang!ley. Lang!ley shows your exact Fortnite Skin, Season Level, and Squad, detects which Minecraft Client & Version you are running, upgrades Steam and other Launcher Games with official Cover Art & Live Stats.

We built Lang!ley as a fast, 100% local, Open Source & Privacy-first Companion so anyone can customize their Discord Rich Presence across all their Games without needing to be a Coder.
