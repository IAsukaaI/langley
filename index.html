ABOUT LANG!LEY

Lang!ley is a lightweight, 100% Open Source Windows Application that brings real-time Fortnite Stats, Squad detection, Friendlist tracking, and Media Controls directly to your Discord Rich Presence.

OPEN BETA & DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Lang!ley is now in Open Beta and still in active Development. Because we are still working on it and adding new Stuff, some Bugs or small Issues can still happen while using the App.

⚠ Note: If you find any Bugs or something doesn't work right, keep in mind that Lang!ley is not finished yet and gets regular Updates on GitHub.

FEATURES & CAPABILITIES

  • • Real-Time Outfit & Locker Sync: Instantly detects your equipped Fortnite Skin and updates your Discord Rich Presence with the exact Skin Icon.
  • • Live Match State, Game Mode & Session Timer: Shows whether you are in the Lobby or In Match, your active Playlist (Battle Royale, Zero Build, Ranked, Reload, OG, Creative, etc.), and your elapsed session Time.
  • • Season Level & Party Size Tracking: Displays your current Battle Pass Season Level and live Party Status (Solo or Party X/4).
  • • Automatic Lang!ley Squad Detection: Detects Teammates in your Fortnite Party who also run Lang!ley (★ Lang!ley Squad) via Epic Games Party Metadata without any external Servers.
  • • Fortnite Friendlist & Squad Highlighter: Displays your Epic Games Friendlist with online Status, current Party Members, and highlights active ★ Lang!ley Squad Mates.
  • • Music Control: Built-in playback Controls (Prev, Play/Pause, Next) supporting Spotify, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Apple Music, and Browser Players — with optional live Song Display inside your Discord RPC Status.
  • • Smart Auto-Updater & System Tray Mode: Notifies you when a new Patch is available (with Changelog Preview and 1-Click install) and runs quietly in the Windows System Tray with optional Autostart (automatically starts with the PC).
  • • No Performance Loss Lang!ley is really lightweight. The highest resource usage of Lang!ley, that we tested was below 50MB RAM.

SECURITY & PRIVACY INFORMATION

  • • 100% Open Source: Lang!ley is completely Open Source on GitHub. Everyone can look at the Code by themselves to see that nothing shady happens in the Background.
  • • Optional Account Linking: Linking your Epic Games Account is completely Optional. Lang!ley works right out of the Box using local Game Logs, while linking unlocks Friendlist, Squad Detection, and instant Profile Syncing.
  • • 100% Local Storage on Your PC: All your Data is stored on your PC, and never gets shared with Others (even the Lang!ley Team can't see anything of you)
  • • Zero External Servers: Lang!ley has no backend Server and never collects, tracks, or shares your Personal Data. All requests go directly between your PC and the Official Epic Games Servers.
  • • Official Browser Login: If you decide, to connect your Epic Games Account, you sign in directly on the Official Epic Games Website in your Browser. Lang!ley never sees your Password or 2FA Codes.
  • • Instant Unlink: You can unlink your Epic Account and remove local Tokens at any time with a single Click.

WHY DID WE BUILD LANG!LEY?

Normally the Rich Prefrence of Fortnite looks really boring on Discord. Thats why we built Lang!ley. Lang!ley shows your Game State, Season Level, Party Size (even with who you play, if you and your Mate use Lang!ley) and Lang!ley even shows the Skin youre using at the Moment.

We built Lang!ley as a fast, 100% local, Open Source & Privacy-first Companion for Peopole who want, that their Discord Rich Prefrence looks a bit nicer. Lang!ley is now in Open Beta so everyone can try it out while we improve Features and fix Bugs.
