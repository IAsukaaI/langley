ABOUT LANG!LEY

Lang!ley is a lightweight, 100% Open Source Windows Application that brings real-time Fortnite Stats, Steam Games, Roblox Experiences, Xbox / PC Game Pass Titles, Squad detection, Cross-Platform Friendlists, and Media Controls directly to your Discord Rich Presence.

OPEN BETA & DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Lang!ley is now in Open Beta and still in active Development. Because we are still working on it and adding new Stuff, some Bugs or small Issues can still happen while using the App.

⚠ Note: If you find any Bugs or something doesn't work right, keep in mind that Lang!ley is not finished yet and gets regular Updates on GitHub.

FEATURES & CAPABILITIES

  • • Fortnite Real-Time Outfit, Match State & Level Sync: Instantly detects your equipped Fortnite Skin (with exact Skin Icon), Lobby/In-Match State, active Playlist, Season Level, Party Size, and elapsed session Time.
  • • Automatic Lang!ley Squad Detection: Detects Teammates in your Fortnite Party who also run Lang!ley (★ Lang!ley Squad) via Epic Games Party Metadata without any external Servers.
  • • Steam Hub, Game Launcher & Live GSI Stats: Automatically detects any running Steam Game with official Cover Art and Session Timer, lets you Launch or Close installed Steam Games with 1 Click, and shows live Match Stats (Map, Score, K/D/A) in Games like Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2.
  • • Roblox & Xbox / PC Game Pass Integration: Automatically detects the exact Roblox Experience you are playing (with official 512x512 Game Icon & Creator) as well as running Xbox / PC Game Pass Games with Cover Art and your Gamertag.
  • • Cross-Platform Friends & Accounts Tab: Link and manage your Epic Games, Steam, Roblox, and Xbox Accounts in one Place and check your Friends' online & in-game Status across Platforms.
  • • Music Control: Built-in playback Controls (Prev, Play/Pause, Next) supporting Spotify, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Apple Music, and Browser Players — with optional live Song Display inside your Discord RPC Status across all Games.
  • • Live RPC Preview, Auto-Updater & System Tray Mode: Features a live Discord Preview right on the Dashboard, 1-Click Auto-Updates with Changelog Preview, and runs quietly in the Windows System Tray with optional Autostart.
  • • No Performance Loss: Lang!ley is really lightweight. The highest resource usage of Lang!ley, that we tested was below 50MB RAM.

SECURITY & PRIVACY INFORMATION

  • • 100% Open Source: Lang!ley is completely Open Source on GitHub. Everyone can look at the Code by themselves to see that nothing shady happens in the Background.
  • • Optional Account Linking: Linking your Accounts is completely Optional. Fortnite, Steam, Roblox, and Xbox Detection work right out of the Box using local Logs and Registry Keys, while linking unlocks Friendlists, Squad Detection, and Profile Names.
  • • 100% Local Storage on Your PC: All your Data is stored on your PC, and never gets shared with Others (even the Lang!ley Team can't see anything of you).
  • • Zero External Servers: Lang!ley has no backend Server and never collects, tracks, or shares your Personal Data. All requests go directly between your PC and the Official Epic Games, Steam, Roblox, and Discord Servers.
  • • Official Browser Login & Safe Linking: If you decide to connect your Epic Games Account, you sign in directly on the Official Epic Games Website in your Browser. Steam, Roblox, and Xbox only use your public Profile ID, Username, or local Client Config — Lang!ley never sees your Passwords or 2FA Codes.
  • • Instant Unlink: You can unlink any Account and remove local Data at any time with a single Click in the Accounts Tab.

WHY DID WE BUILD LANG!LEY?

Normally the Rich Prefrence of Fortnite, Steam, Roblox, or Xbox Games looks really boring on Discord. Thats why we built Lang!ley. Lang!ley shows your exact Fortnite Skin, Game State, Season Level, and Party Size (even with who you play, if you and your Mate use Lang!ley) — and automatically upgrades your Steam, Roblox, and Xbox Games with official Cover Art, Game Icons, Live Stats, and your currently playing Spotify Song.

We built Lang!ley as a fast, 100% local, Open Source & Privacy-first Companion for Peopole who want, that their Discord Rich Prefrence looks a bit nicer across all their Games. Lang!ley is now in Open Beta so everyone can try it out while we improve Features and fix Bugs.

FAQ (OFTEN ASKED QUESTIONS)

Which Games and Platforms are supported? ▼

Lang!ley supports Fortnite (with Skin Icon, Season Level, Mode & Squad Detection), all Steam Games (with Cover Art, Steam Hub Launcher & live CS2/Dota 2 Stats), Roblox (with exact Experience Name & Game Icon), and Xbox / PC Game Pass Games.

Is Lang!ley safe to use? ▼

Yes, 100%. Lang!ley only uses local Game Logs and Registry Keys on your PC and talks directly to the Official Epic Games, Steam, Roblox, and Discord Servers. Because the Code is completely Open Source on GitHub, everyone can check it by themselves.

How much does Lang!ley cost? ▼

Nothing. Lang!ley is 100% free for everyone and there are no Paid Features, Ads, or Subscriptions.

How does Lang!ley earn Money? ▼

It doesn't! Lang!ley is a pure Passion Project built for Fun and for the Community. We don't earn a single Cent with it — there are no Ads, no Paid Versions, no tracking, and we never sell any Data. Because Lang!ley runs 100% locally on your PC without external Backend Servers, it also costs us nothing to keep it free forever.

Can I get banned in Fortnite, Steam, Roblox, or Xbox for using Lang!ley? ▼

No. Lang!ley does not touch or change any Game Files, doesn't read Game Memory, and doesn't inject anything into your Games. It just runs in the Background and reads normal Log Files, Windows Registry States, and official Game State Integrations.

Do I have to link all my Accounts to use Lang!ley? ▼

No. Account Linking in the Accounts Tab is 100% optional. Game Detection for Fortnite, Steam, Roblox, and Xbox works right out of the Box without linking anything. Linking just unlocks extra Features like Friendlists, your Username in Discord RPC, and Fortnite Squad Detection.

Does Lang!ley lower my FPS? ▼

No. Lang!ley runs quietly in the Background and uses under 50MB RAM, so you won't get any FPS Drops while playing.

How do I update Lang!ley? ▼

Lang!ley has a built-in Auto-Updater. When a new Update is out, the App shows a small Update Button at the Top so you can install it with 1 Click.
