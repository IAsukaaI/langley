ABOUT LANG!LEY

Lang!ley~ is a lightweight, modular, 100% Open Source Windows Gaming Companion that brings real-time Fortnite Stats, 11 HoYoPlay & Gacha Games, 5 Minecraft Clients, major PC Launchers, Steam, Roblox, Xbox, and Custom .exe Apps directly to your Discord Rich Presence.

OPEN BETA & DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Lang!ley is currently in Open Beta and in active Development. Regular updates are delivered via its built-in Auto-Updater and on GitHub.

FEATURES & CAPABILITIES

  • • Fortnite Real-Time Stats: Detects equipped skin, mode, level, and squad in real-time across Epic Games and Xbox PC.
  • • 11 Gacha Games (100% Auto-Sync): Automatic level (AR/TL/IK/UL), region, and character sync for Genshin, Star Rail, ZZZ, WuWa, NIKKE, and more.
  • • Browser Login & Auto-Sync: Effortless 1-click account linking for all platforms, Minecraft, and Gacha portals via local browser callback.
  • • Universal Discord Priority Guard: Periodically locks Lang!ley at slot #1 on Discord, overriding background overlays like Medal.tv and Overwolf.
  • • 5 Major Minecraft Clients: Automatic detection for Lunar, Badlion, Feather, LabyMod, and Prism/Vanilla with active versions and servers.
  • • Major PC Launchers & Steam: Full support for Riot Games, Battle.net, EA App, Ubisoft, GOG, Roblox, Xbox, and live CS2/Dota 2 stats.
  • • Custom External .exe Support: Easily add any custom game or application with a custom title, status text, and icon.
  • • Media & Spotify Controller: Built-in playback controls with optional live song display across all games.

SECURITY & PRIVACY INFORMATION

  • • 100% Open Source & Local: Code is fully open source on GitHub. All personal settings and tokens stay isolated locally in %APPDATA%\Langley\ with zero external backend servers.
