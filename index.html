ABOUT LANG!LEY

Lang!ley is a lightweight, 100% Open Source Windows Gaming Companion that brings real-time Fortnite Stats, Minecraft Clients (Lunar, Badlion, Feather, LabyMod & Prism/Vanilla), major PC Launchers (Riot Games, Battle.net, EA App, Ubisoft Connect & GOG Galaxy), Steam Games, Roblox Experiences, and Xbox / PC Game Pass Titles — complete with Medal.tv RPC Priority Guard, Cross-Platform Friendlists, and Media Controls — directly to your Discord Rich Presence.

OPEN BETA & DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Lang!ley is currently in Open Beta (v1.0.3) and in active Development. Because we are constantly expanding Platform Support and adding new Features, some Bugs or small Issues can still happen while using the App.

⚠ Note: If you find any Bugs or something doesn't work right, keep in mind that Lang!ley is still evolving and receives regular Updates via its built-in Auto-Updater and on GitHub.

  • • Unified Game Hub & Live CS2 / Dota 2 GSI Stats: Browse your installed Steam, Xbox Game Pass, and Launcher Libraries in one unified Tab, Launch or Close Steam Games with 1 Click, and broadcast live Match Stats (Map, Score, K/D/A) in Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2.

  • • Media & Spotify Controller: Built-in playback Controls (Prev, Play/Pause, Next) supporting Spotify, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Apple Music, and Browser Players — with optional live Song Display inside your Discord Status across all Games.
  • • Ultra-Low Resource Usage (<50MB RAM): Powered by a shared, TTL-cached OS Process & Window Scanner so all Detectors share a single lightweight scan per cycle with zero FPS Drops.

SECURITY & PRIVACY INFORMATION

  • • 100% Open Source: Lang!ley is completely Open Source on GitHub. Everyone can inspect the Code by themselves to verify that nothing shady happens in the Background.
  • • Optional Account Linking: Linking Accounts in the Accounts Tab is completely Optional. Detection for Fortnite, Minecraft, Riot, Battle.net, EA, Ubisoft, GOG, Steam, Roblox, and Xbox works right out of the Box using local Logs, Manifests, and Registry Keys.
  • • 100% Local Storage on Your PC: All your Settings and Data stay locally on your PC and never get shared with anyone (not even the Lang!ley Team can see anything of you).
  • • Zero External Backend Servers: Lang!ley has no external Backend Server and never collects, tracks, or sells Personal Data. All requests go directly between your PC and Official Game / Discord APIs.
  • • Official Browser Login & Safe Linking: If you connect your Epic Games Account, you sign in directly on the Official Epic Games Website in your Browser. Steam, Roblox, and Xbox only use your public Profile ID, Username, or local Client Config — Lang!ley never sees your Passwords or 2FA Codes.
  • • Instant Unlink & Per-Platform Toggles: You can unlink any Account or toggle individual Game & Minecraft Client RPCs on or off at any time with a single Click.

WHY DID WE BUILD LANG!LEY?

Normally, default Discord Rich Presence for Fortnite, Minecraft, Steam, Roblox, Xbox, or PC Launchers looks either super basic or is completely missing — and background clip recorders like Medal.tv often overwrite your Game Status. That's why we built Lang!ley. Lang!ley shows your exact Fortnite Skin, Season Level, and Squad, detects which Minecraft Client & Version you are running, upgrades Steam, Riot, Battle.net, EA, Ubisoft, GOG, Roblox, and Xbox Games with official Cover Art & Live Stats, and keeps your Game Status locked above Medal.tv.

We built Lang!ley as a fast, 100% local, Open Source & Privacy-first Companion with simple explanations and hover tooltips so anyone can customize their Discord Rich Presence across all their Games without needing to be a Coder.

FAQ (OFTEN ASKED QUESTIONS)

Which Games, Launchers & Minecraft Clients are supported? ▼

Lang!ley supports 10 Rich Presence Networks out of the box: Fortnite (Skin Icon, Level, Mode & Squad), Minecraft (Lunar Client, Badlion, Feather, LabyMod & Prism/Vanilla/Bedrock), Riot Games (VALORANT & League of Legends), Blizzard Battle.net (Call of Duty, WoW, Overwatch 2, Diablo IV, etc.), EA App (Apex Legends, EA Sports FC, Battlefield, Sims 4), Ubisoft Connect (Rainbow Six Siege, XDefiant, Assassin's Creed), GOG Galaxy, all Steam Games (with Game Hub & CS2/Dota 2 Live Stats), Roblox, and Xbox / PC Game Pass.

How does the Medal.tv RPC Priority Guard work? ▼

When Medal.tv runs in the background, it often tries to overwrite your Discord Rich Presence with its own recording status. Lang!ley automatically detects when Medal.tv or MedalEncoder is active and re-asserts exclusive Discord IPC priority without touching or closing Medal's processes, so your Clips keep working while Discord always shows your Game.

Is Lang!ley safe to use? ▼

Yes, 100%. Lang!ley only reads local Game Logs, Manifests, Window Titles, and Registry Keys on your PC and talks directly to Official Game and Discord APIs. Because the Code is completely Open Source on GitHub, everyone can verify it by themselves.

How much does Lang!ley cost & how does it earn Money? ▼

Nothing! Lang!ley is a 100% free Passion Project built for the Community. There are no Paid Features, no Ads, no Subscriptions, and we never sell any Data. Because Lang!ley runs 100% locally on your PC without external Backend Servers, it stays free forever.

Can I get banned in any Game for using Lang!ley? ▼

No. Lang!ley never modifies Game Files, never reads Game Memory, and never injects anything into your Games or Anti-Cheats (Vanguard, Ricochet, EasyAntiCheat, BattlEye). It simply runs in the Background and reads standard Process Names, Log Files, Registry States, and official Game State Integrations.

Do I have to link my Accounts or enable every Platform? ▼

No. Account Linking in the Accounts Tab is 100% optional, and inside the new Settings Tab you can individually toggle any of the 10 Platforms or 5 Minecraft Clients on or off, or pick which Platform takes priority when multiple Games are open.

Does Lang!ley lower my FPS? ▼

No. Lang!ley uses a single shared TTL Process & Window Scanner in the Background and stays under 50MB RAM, ensuring zero FPS Drops while gaming.

How do I update Lang!ley? ▼

Lang!ley has a built-in Auto-Updater. When a new Version is released, an Update Button appears in the Header so you can view the Changelog and install the Update with 1 Click.
