ABOUT LANG!LEY

Lang!ley~ is a lightweight, modular, 100% Open Source Windows Gaming Companion that brings real-time Fortnite Stats (Epic & Xbox PC), 11 HoYoPlay & Gacha Games (Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero, Wuthering Waves, NIKKE & more), 5 Minecraft Clients (Lunar, Badlion, Feather, LabyMod & Prism/Vanilla), major PC Launchers (Riot Games, Battle.net, EA App, Ubisoft Connect & GOG Galaxy), Steam Games, Roblox Experiences, Xbox / PC Game Pass Titles, Custom External (.exe) Apps, Cross-Platform Friendlists, and Media Controls — directly to your Discord Rich Presence.

OPEN BETA & DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Lang!ley is currently in Open Beta and in active Development. Because we are constantly expanding Platform Support and adding new Features, some Bugs or small Issues can still happen while using the App.

⚠ Note: If you find any Bugs or something doesn't work right, keep in mind that Lang!ley is still evolving and receives regular Updates via its built-in Auto-Updater, in-app Patch Notes viewer, and on GitHub.

FEATURES & CAPABILITIES

  • • Fortnite Real-Time Stats: Detects your equipped skin, mode, level, and squad in real-time.
  • • Gacha Games: Automatic level (AR/TL/IK/UL), region, and character sync for Genshin, Star Rail, ZZZ, WuWa, NIKKE, and more.
  • • Browser Login & Auto-Sync: Effortless 1-click account linking for all platforms via local browser callback.
  • • Universal Discord Priority Guard: Periodically locks Lang!ley at slot #1 on Discord, overriding background overlays like Medal.tv and Overwolf.
  • • Major Minecraft Clients: Automatic detection for Lunar, Badlion, Feather, LabyMod, and Prism/Vanilla with active versions and servers.
  • • Major PC Launchers & Steam: Full support for Riot Games, Battle.net, EA App, Ubisoft, GOG, Roblox, Xbox, and live CS2/Dota 2 stats.
  • • Custom External Game and App Support: Easily add any custom Game or Application with a custom title, status text, and icon.
  • • Media & Spotify Controller: Built-in playback controls with optional live song display across all games.

SECURITY & PRIVACY INFORMATION

  • • 100% Open Source & Modular: Lang!ley is completely Open Source on GitHub so everyone can inspect the Code by themselves.
  • • Isolated Local Storage: All your personal Settings, linked Account IDs, and Custom Apps are stored strictly locally on your PC inside %APPDATA%\Lang!ley\ and are never shared with anyone.
  • • Zero External Backend Servers: Lang!ley has no external Backend Server and never collects, tracks, or sells Personal Data. All requests go directly between your PC and Official Game / Discord APIs.
  • • Safe Official Browser Login & Local Auto-Sync: When linking accounts via Browser Login, you sign in on the official platform Websites in your own default Browser while Lang!ley's local loopback callback (`127.0.0.1`) and local log/registry readers sync your public profile data — Lang!ley never sees your Passwords or 2FA Codes.
  • • Instant Unlink & Per-Game Toggles: You can unlink any Account or toggle individual Platforms, Gacha Games, and Minecraft Clients on or off at any time with a single Click.

FAQ (OFTEN ASKED QUESTIONS)

Which Games, Launchers & Gacha Titles are supported? ▼

Lang!ley supports Fortnite, Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero, Honkai Impact 3rd, Wuthering Waves, NIKKE, Reverse: 1999, Punishing: Gray Raven, Solo Leveling:ARISE, Infinity Nikki, Arknights: Endfield, Major Minecraft Clients, Riot Games, Blizzard (Battle.net), EA App, Ubisoft Connect, GOG Galaxy, Steam, Roblox, Xbox / PC Game Pass, plus any Custom External Game or App you add.

How does the Discord Priority Guard work? ▼

When overlays (like Medal.tv, Overwolf, GeForce Overlay, SteelSeries GG, Razer Cortex, or PreMiD) or built-in game RPCs run in the background, they often try to overwrite your Discord status. Lang!ley automatically sends periodic IPC promotion pulses so Discord always keeps Lang!ley locked in slot #1 without modifying or closing any of your background apps.

How do Gacha Game Levels & Characters get detected? ▼

100% automatically! Lang!ley uses its Browser Login callback, standard local game logs, registry keys, and public UID showcase APIs to automatically display your Adventure Rank (AR), Trailblaze Level (TL), Inter-Knot Level (IK Lv.), or Union Level (UL), Server Region, and active Character.

Can I get banned in any Game for using Lang!ley? ▼

No. Lang!ley never modifies Game Files, never reads Game Memory, and never injects anything into your Games or Anti-Cheats (Vanguard, Ricochet, EasyAntiCheat, BattlEye, HoYoProtect, ACE). It simply runs in the Background and reads standard Process Names, visible Window Titles, Log Files, Registry States, and official APIs.

How much does Lang!ley cost & is my Data safe? ▼

Lang!ley is 100% free and Open Source forever — no Paid Features, no Ads, and no Backend Servers. All personal configuration files stay isolated in your local %APPDATA%\Langley\ folder on your PC. Even the Lang!ley Team cant see anything of you!

How do I update Lang!ley or read Patch Notes? ▼

Lang!ley includes a built-in Auto-Updater in the Header whenever a new release drops, plus a dedicated Patch Notes button in the bottom bar so you can view the full changelog history inside the app at any time.
