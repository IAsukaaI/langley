ABOUT LANG!LEY // CLOSED BETA

Lang!ley is a lightweight Windows background application that connects your live Fortnite session to Discord Rich Presence—displaying your equipped locker outfit, Season Level, active playlist, and party squad in real time.

⚠ Development Notice: Lang!ley is currently still in active development. Public downloads are disabled and only selected testers receive access to the installer.

CORE FEATURES
Live Locker Outfit (PNG)
Automatically resolves and displays your equipped Fortnite skin icon in Discord.
Lang!ley Squad Detection
Shows live party size (e.g. 2/4) and highlights teammates who also use Lang!ley.
Season Level & Game Mode
Displays your Epic username, Season Level, and current playlist in Lobby or Match.
Lightweight System Tray App
Runs silently in the background with a live session timer and auto-hide when offline.
DATA STORAGE & PRIVACY
  • • 100% Local Storage on Your PC: Your authentication token and settings are stored exclusively on your own computer in your personal Windows Registry (HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\LangleyFortniteRPC).
  • • Zero External Servers: Lang!ley has no backend server and never collects, uploads, or shares your data. All requests go directly between your PC and official Epic Games servers (*.epicgames.com).
  • • Official Browser Login: You sign in directly on the official Epic Games website in your browser. Lang!ley never sees your password or 2FA codes.
  • • Instant Unlink: You can unlink your account at any time with a single click, which immediately deletes the local token.
  • • Epic not required: Linking your Epic Games to Lang!ley is fully optional and not required. If you dont feel Safe about it (Some Features do not work then).
